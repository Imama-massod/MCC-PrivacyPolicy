Delete Account and Data

App: MCC – Milk Collection (package com.flow.mcc)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

MCC accounts are created and managed by your milk collection centre. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to usama.munawar@flowtecz.com with the subject MCC account deletion request.
  2. Include your name, your centre's name and the username or phone number you use to sign in, so we can find your account.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request really comes from you. Centre accounts may also need approval from your centre administrator.
  4. We will delete the account and confirm by email.

You can also ask your centre administrator to remove your account directly.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, PIN and role or permission settings.
  • Personal details linked to your account, such as your name and contact details.

What may be kept

  • Business records that your centre needs for its accounts and legal duties, such as milk collection entries, payments and balances, may be kept by the centre. Where possible, they are kept without your personal details.
  • Backups and logs may keep data for a short time before they are removed in the normal cycle.

Remove only some data

To correct or remove only some of your data without deleting the whole account, use the same email and say which information you want changed.
