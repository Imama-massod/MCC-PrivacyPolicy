Privacy Policy

App: MCC – Milk Collection (package com.flow.mcc)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MCC – Milk Collection mobile app ("the App") handles information. The App is used by milk collection centres to record milk collection, manage farmers and payments, and view reports.

1. Information the App handles

The App does not collect information for advertising or analytics. It only handles data that your milk collection centre enters or that you need to sign in:

  • Account information: your username or PIN and role, used to sign in and decide what you can see.
  • Business records: farmer and supplier names and contact details, milk collection entries (quantity, fat and similar readings), rates, payments, balances and reports.

2. Where the data is stored

The App does not keep a separate database of its own. It connects over a secure internet connection to your centre's own MCC system, and your records are stored in that system's database, which is managed by your centre. A small amount of data, such as your sign-in session, is kept on your device so you stay signed in.

3. How the information is used

  • To sign you in and apply your role and permissions.
  • To record, show and report milk collection and payment data.
  • To create receipts and reports that you choose to print or share.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with your milk collection centre's system, and with other apps only when you choose to share a receipt or report (for example through WhatsApp).

5. Security

Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access is limited by user login and role. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Records are kept by your milk collection centre for as long as it needs them for its business. To correct or delete your data or account, contact your centre administrator or us using the details below.

7. Children

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

8. Device permissions

The App needs internet access to work. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone for tracking.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its date are always shown on this page.

10. Contact
